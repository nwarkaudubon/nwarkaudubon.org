The Northwest Arkansas Audubon Society, in partnership with Donna and Kelly Mulhollan, raised money to buy binoculars for kids. The goal was to put a pair of binoculars in the hands of aspiring young naturalists and birders.

## Application guidelines (2023-24 round)

- Youth in the 8-12 age group could apply for the binoculars.
- Only available to youth in Benton, Carroll, Madison, and Washington County.
- Youth had to submit hand-written answers to application questions.
- No more than 10 pairs of binoculars would be awarded that round to those youth deemed suitable for receipt of binoculars.
- NWAAS reserved the right to not award all 10 pairs if the committee deemed applications inadequate.
- Membership in NWAAS was encouraged.
- Recipients were asked to record birds through the use of field notes. Each winner received directions on binocular use, info on creating a Life List, how to use eBird to track bird sightings, and a copy of Toucan Jam's THINGS WITH WINGS CD.
- A committee of Donna and Kelly Mulhollan and Joe Neal read and ranked the applications based on merit.
- **The deadline for applications was January 1, 2024.**
