Here are a few ways to get started learning about the birds of northwestern Arkansas.

1. **Get involved in our field trips.** They are free and open to everyone. See the [upcoming field trips](/field-trips/).
2. **Read the Arkansas Audubon Society field list.** Arkansas Audubon publishes a handy annotated field list to the birds of Arkansas. It includes birds from throughout the state, so some of it is not directly relevant to northwestern Arkansas, but most of it provides good background on our birds. Read the [Arkansas Audubon Field List 2009](http://www.arbirds.org/aas_fieldlist09.pdf) (PDF), and see [species added to the list since 2009](http://www.arbirds.org/photos_brc.html).
3. **Read Joe Neal's books.** A PDF of *Birds in Northwestern Arkansas: an ecological perspective* is on our [books page](/resources/books/).
4. **Find out how common a bird is.** Check out Mike Mlodinow's [frequency status designations](/documents/resources/regional-e-bird-frequencies-key-to-status-mlodinow-oct-2012.pdf) (PDF) for northwest Arkansas and Arkansas in general. Read an [explanation](/documents/resources/mike-mlodinow-explains-how-he-came-up-with-values-for-the-status-of-birds-in-arkansas.pdf) (PDF) of how Mike analyzed real bird data to come up with his designations.
