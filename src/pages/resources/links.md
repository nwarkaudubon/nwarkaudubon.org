## Important Bird Areas

Arkansas has many places that birders have visited over the years. Some have special importance. These have been recognized as Important Bird Areas (IBAs) by both National Audubon and its Arkansas affiliate, Audubon Arkansas. You can get more information about IBAs in general, and find IBAs in Arkansas, including those in northwest Arkansas, at [ar.audubon.org/important-bird-areas-arkansas](http://ar.audubon.org/important-bird-areas-arkansas).
